05.06.2026
NEW SERVICE IN CORPORATE INTERNET BANKING: Ruble transfers between own accounts during official holidays in the Russian Federation.

Dear Clients! We are pleased to inform you that the capabilities of our Corporate Internet Banking system have been expanded. You can now execute transfers in Russian Rubles between your own accounts (including deposit top-ups and partial withdrawals) even during official public holidays in the Russian Federation. Please note an important condition: If a public holiday in Russia coincides with a weekend or a public holiday in Kazakhstan, these transfers will not be available, as they require the operation of the currency control department. How it works (Examples): • June 12 (a holiday in the Russian Federation, but a business day in the Republic of Kazakhstan) — transfers between own accounts in Rubles are available. • May 1 (a holiday in the Russian Federation and the Republic of Kazakhstan) — transfers between own accounts in Rubles are not available. The processing procedure for all other Ruble transactions during Russian holidays remains unchanged. This new feature is already live and available in your Corporate Internet Banking System. Best regards, Subsidiary JSC VTB Bank (Kazakhstan)

Dear Customers!

We are pleased to inform you about the launch of a new service in the "Internet Banking for Legal Entities" system: "Request for Withdrawal/Disbursement of Cash from a Bank Account."

This new service allows you to submit a Request for withdrawal/disbursement of cash from a bank account via the IBLE system. The request form is pre-filled with data, so you only need to manually fill in two fields: the date of withdrawal and the amount of money you plan to withdraw.
